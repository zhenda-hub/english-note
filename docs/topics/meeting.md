# 会议与公司动态（Meetings & Company Updates）

## 会议开场与流程

- Thanks for coming! I'm glad you're here. 谢谢大家来！很高兴见到你们。
- Let's get started. 我们开始吧。
- meeting host 会议主持人 / meeting attendee 与会者
- agenda: topics of the meeting 议程：会议要讨论的议题
- action item: next step 待办事项：下一步要做的
- that's it then 那就这些了（会议收尾）

## 保密与纪律

- confidential = keep it quiet 机密的，要保密
- be quiet 保持安静

## 经营状况表达

- purchase 购买 / 采购
- revenue 收入 / costs 成本 / expenses 开支 / profits 利润 / market share 市场份额
- 走势：rose 上升了 / fell 下降了 / significantly 显著地 → Revenue has risen significantly. 收入显著增长。
- There are layoffs. 有裁员。
- cut the budget 削减预算
- hurt employee morale 打击员工士气
- cut down on … / reduce 削减、减少

## 公司动态与传闻

- We're kicking off a new project in the second quarter. 我们将在第二季度启动一个新项目。
- Sales is recruiting a new team to drive growth. 销售部正在招新团队来推动增长。
- We're making some staff redundant. 我们正在裁员。
- Our team is expanding, and we're moving to new offices. 团队在扩大，我们要搬去新办公室。
- The company is downsizing the sales department. 公司正在缩减销售部门。
- There's a rumor going around that we're kicking off a new project. 有传言说我们要启动一个新项目。
