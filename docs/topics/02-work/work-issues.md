# 职场问题与请假（Workplace Issues & Time Off）

## 守时与效率

- He never meets deadlines. 他从不按时完成任务。
- meeting deadlines 按时完成任务
- tardiness 迟到
- sloppy 马虎的 / be tidy 保持整洁
- distracted: not focused 分心、不专注
- work late 加班到很晚 / work overtime 加班
- a lot of work 工作量很大
- a work planner 工作计划表
- set three alarms 设三个闹钟（提醒自己起床/别迟到）

## 坦诚沟通

- Can I be honest with you? 我能跟你说实话吗？
- I appreciate your honesty. 我感谢你的坦诚。
- That's pretty radical. 那太激进了。
- What's the story? / What's going on? 发生什么事了？

## 设施故障：东西坏了

- The bathrooms are out of order. 洗手间故障、暂停使用。
- The phone lines are down. 电话线路断了。
- The projector is out of action. 投影仪坏了。
- IT told me that the intranet server is down today. IT 说今天内网服务器宕机了。

> out of order / (be) down / out of action 都可以表示“设备坏了、没法用”。

## 请假与休假

- take time off = 请假 / 休假
- I've got to get right back to the office. 我得马上赶回办公室。

## 加班文化与工作氛围

- We don't go out for fun together — we just work and go home. 我们不一起出去玩，就是上班、回家。
- In China, some companies have a 996 work culture. It means working from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., six days a week. That's 72 hours a week, including breaks.
  - 996 工作制：早 9 点到晚 9 点、一周 6 天，合计每周 72 小时（含休息时间）。
  - work culture 工作文化 / including breaks 含休息时间
- go out for fun 出去玩 / go home 回家（工作—回家两点一线）

## 其他常用词

- arrange 安排
