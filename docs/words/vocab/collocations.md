# 常见动词搭配（Collocations）

## 因果表达：cause / lead to / be due to

- **cause + 名词** = 直接引起 → Working too much can cause anxiety. 工作过度会引起焦虑。
- **lead to + 名词** = 导致（常指间接、渐进的结果）→ Working too much can lead to anxiety.
- **be caused by …** = 由…引起（被动）→ Anxiety can be caused by working too much.
- **be due to …** = 归因于…（后接名词，不接从句）→ Anxiety can be due to working too much.
- **result in / result from** = 导致／源于 → Overwork results in anxiety. / Anxiety results from overwork.

| 结构                | 中文         | 例句                            |
| ------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------- |
| cause sth           | 引起某事     | Stress causes illness.          |
| lead to sth         | 导致某事     | Stress leads to illness.        |
| sth be caused by …  | 某事由…引起  | Illness can be caused by stress.|
| sth be due to …     | 某事归因于…  | Illness can be due to stress.   |

✅ 小技巧：主动句用 cause / lead to，被动句用 be caused by / be due to。
