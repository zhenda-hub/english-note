    动词语法

    约 1235 个字 预计阅读时间 4 分钟

    动词分类¶

    实义动词¶

    表示“做什么 / 发生什么”：

    run, eat, think, work

    系动词（连接用）¶

    be, seem, look, become

    助动词¶

    作用：

    • 构成时态
    • 构成否定
    • 构成疑问

    be / do / have

    情态动词（态度表达）¶

    • can / could（能力、许可、可能性）
    • may / might（许可、可能性）
    • must（必须、强烈推测）
    • shall / should（应当、建议）
    • will / would（将来、礼貌请求）
    • ought to（应当）

    特点： - 后接动词原形（无 to） - 无时态变化（第三人称不变） - 否定加 not（cannot, shouldn't）

    示例： - Can you help me?（你能帮我吗？） - You should study harder.（你应该更努力学习。） - It might rain.（可能要下雨。）

    动词语法¶

    被动语法¶

    be + 过去分词

    need -> be needed

    第三人称单数¶

    have -> has

    只影响一般现在时，其他时态不用纠结。

    动词的时态¶

    过去分词: 动词 + ed

    用法 结构 例子
    现在完成时 have/has + 过去分词 I have eaten
    被动语态 be + 过去分词 The homework was done
    作形容词 过去分词修饰名词 a broken window

    实际上英语真正常用的时态只有：

    现在 / 过去 / 将来 × 简单 + 进行 + 完成 = 9 种常用。

    简单时： I eat / I ate / I will eat 进行时： I am eating / I was eating / I will be eating 完成时： I have eaten / I had eaten / I will have eaten 这 9 个覆盖 99% 的实际使用场景。

    ⏰ 时间 🌱 核心时态（90% 用于日常） 🌿 常用补充（10%） 🪶 很少使用（可略）
    现在 (Present) ✅ 一般现在时 → I eat. ⚪ 现在完成进行时 → I have been eating.
    ✅ 现在进行时(be doing)[动作正在进行] → I am eating.
    ✅ 现在完成时(have done) → I have eaten.
    过去 (Past) ✅ 一般过去时 → I ate. ☑️ 过去完成时 → I had eaten. ⚪ 过去完成进行时 → I had been eating.
    ☑️ 过去进行时 → I was eating.
    将来 (Future) ✅ 一般将来时 → I will eat. ☑️ 将来进行时 → I will be eating. ⚪ 将来完成进行时 → I will have been eating.
    ☑️ 将来完成时 → I will have eaten.

    have done for have done since

    过去现在的时间描述:

    • The party will begin in 10 minutes.
    • The party started 10 minutes ago.

    非谓语动词¶

    当动词不是句子的**主要谓语**

    形式 名称 示例 用途
    to do 不定式 to learn 目的 / 计划
    doing 动名词 / 现在分词 learning 动作 / 过程
    done 过去分词 learned 被动 / 完成

    Learning English is important.

    真正的谓语是 is， 而不是 learning。

    短语动词（Phrasal Verbs）¶

    短语动词是由动词 + 介词/副词颗粒组成，含义常与原动词不同，非常常见于口语。

    短语动词数量众多，需要记忆

    短语动词 中文含义 例句
    look after 照顾 She looks after her younger brother.
    look up 查找（字典等） Look up the word in the dictionary.
    look for 寻找 I'm looking for my keys.
    look forward to 期待 I look forward to seeing you.
    look out 小心 Look out! There's a car coming.
    look at 看 Look at this.
    turn on 打开（灯、水等） Turn on the light, it's dark.
    turn off 关闭 Please turn off the TV.
    turn up 出现；调大声音 He finally turned up at the party.
    get up 起床 I get up at 7 every morning.
    get on 上车；相处 Get on the bus quickly.
    get along 相处融洽 Do you get along with your neighbors?
    put on 穿上；上演 Put on your coat, it's cold outside.
    put off 推迟 Don't put off your homework.
    go on 继续 Go on, tell me more.
    go out 出去；熄灭 Let's go out for dinner.
    come back 回来 When will you come back?
    come up with 想出 She came up with a great idea.
    break down 出故障；崩溃 My car broke down on the highway.
    break up 分手；分解 They broke up last month.
    run out 用完 We've run out of milk.
    run into 偶遇 I ran into an old friend yesterday.
    give up 放弃 Don't give up on your dreams.
    pick up 捡起；接人；学会 Can you pick me up at the station?
    find out 发现；查明 I found out the truth yesterday.
    grow up 长大 What do you want to be when you grow up?
    take off 起飞；脱下 The plane takes off at 8 PM.
    wake up 醒来 Wake up! It's time for school.
    hang up 挂断电话 She hung up without saying goodbye.
    set up 建立；设置 They set up a new company.
    call back 回电话 I'll call you back later.
    make up 化妆；编造；和好 They made up after the fight.
    carry on 继续 Carry on with your work.
    fall asleep 入睡 I fell asleep during the movie.
    work out 解决；锻炼 Let's work out at the gym.
    hold on 等一下；抓住 Hold on, I'll be right there.
    pay back 偿还 I'll pay you back next week.
    check out 检查；结账离开 Check out the new book.
    sit down 坐下 Please sit down and relax.
    stand up 站起来 Stand up when the teacher enters.
    ask out 邀请约会 He asked her out on a date.
    bring up 抚养；提起 Don't bring up that topic again.
    catch up 赶上 I need to catch up on my reading.
    clean up 清理 Clean up your room!
    cut down 减少；砍倒 Cut down on sugar for better health.
    fill in 填写 Fill in the form carefully.
    hand in 交上 Hand in your assignment by Friday.
    let down 让...失望 Don't let me down.
    point out 指出 He pointed out my mistake.
    show up 出现 He didn't show up for the meeting.
    try on 试穿 Try on this dress.
    wait for 等待 I'm waiting for you.
